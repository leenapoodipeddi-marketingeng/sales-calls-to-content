# Positioning Brief — SAMPLE (fictional data)
*Demonstration output. Based on 2 sample calls: Northwind Traders, Contoso Inc.*
*This is illustrative sample output, not real customer data.*

---

## 1. Top Pain Points (ranked by frequency)

**1. Poor support from the incumbent vendor**
*2 of 2 calls*
- Tickets sit for days while work stops (Northwind)
- Fear of being "a small account" ignored in a big vendor's support queue (Contoso)

**2. Unpredictable pricing**
*1 of 2 calls — flagged for intensity*
- "I never know what we're going to pay" (Northwind)

**3. Single point of failure**
*1 of 2 calls*
- Aging system maintained by one soon-to-retire employee (Contoso)

**4. Migration is scary, especially near a busy season / deadline**
*2 of 2 calls*
- Nervous about switching during busy season (Northwind)
- Hard fall deadline; worried about botching a migration (Contoso)

---

## 2. The Language They Use
- "support" / "picks up the phone"
- "white glove"
- "single point of failure"
- "I never know what we're going to pay"
- "small account"
- "botched migration"

---

## 3. Objections & Hesitations
- Timing: both prospects fear disruption during a critical window.
- Vendor size: worry a big provider won't prioritize a small account.
- Migration risk: switching feels dangerous because they're mid-operation.

---

## 4. Desired Outcomes
- A human who answers when things break.
- Predictable monthly cost.
- A safe migration that doesn't threaten customer relationships.
- Less reliance on one irreplaceable person.

---

## 5. One-Line Positioning Hypothesis
> "When your current vendor leaves you stuck, over-charged, and unsupported,
> we're the team that actually picks up the phone — and gets you switched
> without breaking your business."

*Hypothesis only — to be tested against a broader prospect set.*
