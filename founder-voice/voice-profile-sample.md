# Voice Profile — SAMPLE (fictional)

*This is an illustrative sample profile for a made-up founder, "Alex Rivera,"
so the pipeline can be demonstrated without using anyone's real writing. Build
your own with `build_voice.py` from real writing samples.*

---

## 1. Overall Voice
Alex comes across as a practical operator who has made the mistakes they're
warning you about. The voice is direct and unpolished on purpose — more "let me
save you some pain" than "let me impress you." Confidence without swagger.

## 2. Tone & Attitude
Diagnostic and a little contrarian. Alex names the thing everyone's thinking but
not saying, then reframes it. Not alarmist, but there's a quiet urgency — the
sense that the status quo is quietly costing you. Ends by pulling the reader into
a conversation rather than closing a sale.

## 3. Sentence & Structure Patterns
- Short sentences. Rarely complex.
- One- or two-line paragraphs. White space is deliberate.
- Opens with a hook that states a hard truth flatly.
- Uses arrow-stepped lists (→) to show a process or stack a point.
- Fragments for emphasis. "Every time. From scratch."
- Closes with a question to the reader.

## 4. Vocabulary & Phrasing
Plain, concrete, zero corporate jargon. Avoids "leverage," "synergy," "in
today's fast-paced world." Reaches for everyday words. Occasional dry humor.
No emojis except an occasional single one to point (👇). Never hashtag spam.

## 5. Quirks & Tells
- Starts posts mid-thought, like continuing a conversation.
- Admits the unglamorous truth ("I was doing this by hand, badly").
- Reframes a fear into a bigger fear ("switching feels risky — staying is the
  real risk").
- The takeaway is usually implied, not stated.

## 6. Do / Don't Rules
DO:
- Lead with a flat, specific hard truth.
- Keep paragraphs to one or two lines.
- Use → lists to show process or stack an argument.
- Use fragments for punch.
- End with a genuine question.
- Sound like a person talking, not a brand publishing.

DON'T:
- Use corporate jargon or hype words.
- Write long, winding paragraphs.
- Over-explain the moral of the story.
- Stack hashtags.
- Sound salesy or self-congratulatory.
